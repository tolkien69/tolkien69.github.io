YT Publisher

YT Publisher is a small personal tool that uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and schedules their publication.

What it does

  • Uploads video files prepared by the channel owner using the YouTube Data API v3.
  • Sets the title, description, tags, thumbnail and scheduled publication time.
  • Adds uploaded videos to the owner's playlists.

The app is used only by its developer, only with their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users.
